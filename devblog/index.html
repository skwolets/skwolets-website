ABOUT THE DEVELOPMENT

KEY MILESTONES IN THE CREATION OF THE GAME

Here I publish a digest of significant development milestones: new systems, gameplay mechanics and visual improvements. I only show what is actually finished and working.

Next update: when there is something worth showing.

02

THE IDEA

WHY THIS GAME EXISTS IN THE FIRST PLACE

I am making the game I would want to play myself. Sounds cliché? Maybe. But when I looked at modern AAA projects, there was always something that pissed me off: too casual, too shallow, poor performance, network lag, barely any content. The list could go on forever.

If nobody is making the game I want to play — I'll make it myself.

03

THE GOAL

NOT JUST MAKING A GAME

My goal is to create a quality product that:

  • Runs smoothly on average hardware — optimization is not a luxury, it's respect for the player.
  • Provides fair gameplay.
  • Is stable online.
  • Has enough depth to reveal itself after 100+ hours.
  • Still remains accessible and understandable from the first minutes.

I'm not trying to outdo the popular zombie hits. I'm trying to make my own game that can stand alongside them in terms of online presence — but with soul, detail and attention to all the little things that are so often missing from big projects.

I'm not promising a revolution, comrades. I'm promising honest work. Every day I make the game a little better. It's not fast, it's not cheap — at least when it comes to nerves and time — and it doesn't always look pretty during development. But it works.

04

Why am I writing this? Because a good game isn't born in a vacuum. I'm making it alone, but the final product is made for you. Your opinion is the fuel that helps me stay on course.
